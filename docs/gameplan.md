# Blades & Banners: Two Fronts

## Pitch

One hero. Two battlefields. Fight on a ridge to fund the army advancing below, and reclaim five lands from the Ashen Legion.

## Core gameplay loop

Move and dodge → strike upper-field enemies → earn gold → recruit lower-field troops → destroy the opposing stronghold → earn campaign marks → upgrade → advance.

## Game rules

Both battlefields run simultaneously in a fixed side view. Hero movement is restricted to the ridge. Gold starts at 75 and gains 1 every 2 seconds. Four hero enemy classes and six army classes per side have actual combat state. Six repeatable purchases have individual cooldowns. Shieldbearers intercept arrows; priests heal; knights charge; golems deal splash and siege damage. Three formation ranks and separation preserve readability.
Five levels: The Borderlands; The Broken Road; The Marsh Crossing; The Black Forest; Siege of the Ashen Citadel. The final battle introduces Marshal Veyr after 70 seconds. Typical target 2–4 minutes; no hard time gate.

## Win / lose conditions

Destroy the enemy's 100-HP stronghold to win. Hero death or friendly stronghold destruction loses. Victory rewards marks, stars and unlocks. Rewards are first-clear plus improved-star bonuses, preventing upgrade grinding. Local campaign saves persist automatically.

## Art style

Original illustrated 2D vector fantasy with layered mountains, trees, terrain, keeps and articulated character animation. Dawnward palette: steel blue, cream, gold. Ashen palette: charcoal, dark red, muted green. Five distinct landscape palettes and silhouettes. No borrowed franchise assets.

## Audio direction

Original Web Audio synthesized sword, impact, coin, recruitment, arrow, healing, outcome effects plus a quiet modal fantasy music loop. User gesture unlock; mute, music and volume controls.

## Player goals

Short term: balance active combat and recruitment. Long term: clear all five battles and improve star ratings with combined armies and six capped upgrade tracks.

## Anti-goals

No backend, monetization, multiplayer, platforming, hero entry into the lower battle, copied artwork or code.
